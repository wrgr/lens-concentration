## What stays fixed

- Course title and numbers: Principles of Learning Engineering for Next-Generation Systems, ED.893.660 / ED.893.860.
- Fifteen teaching weeks, five CLO statements, and the adopted concentration objectives.
- Participation 25%, Module 1 assignment 15%, Capability Measurement Plan 15%, Iteration Cycle Report 20%, and Iteration Briefing 25%, on a 200-point scale.
- Major deadlines: Weeks 2, 9, 13, and 15.
- Module-level MLOs; no separate weekly objective tier.
- Course-owned AI policy and its reasoning-and-evidence assessment.

## Authoring and casebook resources

Course development remains in `lens-private`. Each module has separate overview, readings, lectures, cases, activities, instructor-guide, and build-checklist sources. Assembly produces the existing four-page Canvas handoff, preserving its BEGIN/END markers. Assignments have separate briefs and rubrics; the AI policy is authored once and quoted unchanged in exports.

Capability Matters explorations are integrated into existing weekly activities. Students navigate the complete edition, domain/course indexes, reference appendix, and LENS Companion; examine evidence and disclosures; compare mechanisms; and apply a result to their own system. These steps add no separate submission or grade allocation.

The casebook quarantine audit excludes Theranos from published editions. Week 7 therefore uses **Case 5, Epic Sepsis Model**, stable slug `epic-sepsis-model`, the audit’s recommended alternative, to examine independent validation in the intended operating setting. This is a course-use selection; it does not retag the canonical casebook’s course mappings. Weeks 3 and 4 retain the practice-synthesis-tier flags on their assigned cases; Week 9 retains the PBIS institutional-overlap disclosure.
