# Pipeline terminology and PR reconciliation

On October 16, 2026, the course owner confirmed **Map** as the second stage. On October 19, **Experiment** replaced Deploy as the sixth stage. Current teaching follows **Understand → Map → Design → Build → Instrument → Experiment → Evaluate → Understand**, with **8. Refine** at the center throughout. Deployment connects the resulting Capability back to the Challenge through adoption and effective use.

Map identifies people, components, interfaces, dependencies, conditions and feedback. Models may support the map; the words are not interchangeable. Experiment is a bounded test with evidence, permissions and stopping rules. Deployment is the separate decision to put the resulting capability to work. Historical records and quotations retain their original labels, as do stable data keys used to read saved journals.

The current program mapping and LEN01 syllabus use these labels in their cycle passages. The objective hierarchy, objective mappings, assessment weights, module boundaries and ordinary technical uses of model and deployment are retained. This records the course owner's direction and does not claim a new CPC or program-director approval.
